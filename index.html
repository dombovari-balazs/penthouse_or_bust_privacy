Penthouse or Bust

Privacy Policy · Effective 27 October 2026

The short version: the game has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or any other personal information. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads, based on the choices you make in the consent and tracking prompts.

Who we are

Penthouse or Bust ("the app") is made by Dombovári Balázs, an independent developer ("we", "us"). We are the controller of any personal data processed through the app. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, floors, upgrades, badges, settings, the time you last played) and your ad settings are saved only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded videos and interstitial ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, otherwise the identifier for vendors (IDFV)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (views, taps, rewards) and app usage related to ads
  • Diagnostics such as crash and performance data of the ads SDK, device model and OS version

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: consent and tracking

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form. You can accept, decline or pick specific purposes. You can change your choice at any time under Office → Privacy choices.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the app asks whether it may track you across apps and websites. If you say no, the IDFA is not shared and you see non-personalised ads. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The game plays the same whatever you choose.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether you own the purchase. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ad settings file

The app may download a small settings file (which ads are switched on) from this website. Like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log your IP address for security purposes; we don't use these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No analytics SDK of our own and no selling of personal data.
  • No real-money gambling: casino floors use play money only and chips cannot be bought.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Personalised ads and storing/accessing information on your device for ads rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, frequency capping and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

Children

The app is rated for adults (17+/18+) because of simulated gambling and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google (Google data & privacy). You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Hungary: NAIH).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
